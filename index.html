01The app

About Panda Music Player Pro

Panda Music Player Pro is an offline music player for Android. It finds the audio files already stored on your phone and plays them — with gapless playback, crossfade, a sleep timer, playlists, favourites, custom themes and synced lyrics.

Playback works with the internet switched off. The app doesn't stream music, doesn't have a catalogue, and can't play anything you don't already have a file for.

Premium is optional, bought through Google Play. It removes ads and unlocks extras like synced lyrics, premium app icons, accent colours and full-screen wallpapers. Everything else works the same either way.

02Collection

What we collect about you

Nothing. There's no sign-up, no login, no profile, and no server of ours that stores your data. The app has no analytics and no crash reporting. We can't see your songs, your playlists, what you listen to, or who you are.

If you email us, we'll have your email address and whatever you write — that's it, and only for as long as it takes to help you.

What the app keeps on your device

All of this lives in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is uploaded:

  • A list of the music files found on your phone, so your library loads instantly
  • Your playlists and favourites
  • Play counts, recently played and recently added
  • Songs you've hidden, and songs in Recently deleted
  • Titles and cover images you've edited yourself
  • Your settings — theme, accent colour, language, crossfade, sleep timer, app icon

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Your actual music files aren't touched by an uninstall — they're yours, and they stay where they are.

03Outbound

The four things that leave your phone

1. Lyrics lookup

When the app fetches lyrics for a song, it sends the song title and artist name to LRCLIB, a free open lyrics database, and gets the lyrics back. Nothing else about you is sent, and no account or key is involved. As with any web request, LRCLIB's servers see your IP address.

2. Ads

If you're not on Premium, ads come from Google AdMob. To choose an ad, AdMob receives your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general device and app information. That's Google's standard ad request — we don't add anything to it and we don't get a copy of it. Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. App notices

The app uses Firebase (a Google service) for two things: it downloads small pieces of configuration — an announcement banner, an update notice, the link to this page — and it registers an anonymous notification token so those notices can reach your app. Every install joins one shared topic, so there's no per-person targeting and no identity attached. Firebase's servers see your IP address and that token. The app never uploads anything to them.

4. Premium purchases

Buying Premium goes through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment — we never see your card, address or Google account. The app simply asks Google whether Premium is active on this device and switches the features on.

04Permissions

Every permission, and why it's there

Android shows you a list of permissions. Here's what each one actually does in this app.

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Find and play the music files on your phone. Without this there's nothing to play. The app only reads audio.

INTERNET
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Fetch lyrics, load ads, receive app notices, and check Premium status. Playing music needs none of it.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Keep the music playing when you leave the app, with the controls in your notification shade.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Show the playback controls, and app notices if you allow them. You can turn notices off and keep the controls.

WAKE_LOCK

Stop the phone from sleeping in the middle of a song.

WRITE_SETTINGS

Used only when you tap “Set as ringtone”. Android asks you separately before it's granted.

AD_ID

Lets AdMob read the advertising ID to serve ads. Unused once you're on Premium.

BILLING

Buy and restore Premium through Google Play.

05Your controls

What you can turn off

  • Ads — buy Premium and the ad requests stop entirely.
  • Advertising ID — Android Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you reset or delete it.
  • App notices — turn them off in Notification settings inside the app, or in Android's settings. Playback controls keep working.
  • Lyrics lookups — they only happen while you're using the lyrics view. Go offline and the app plays without contacting anything.
  • Music access — revoke it in Android Settings → Apps → Panda Music Player Pro → Permissions.
  • Everything — uninstall, and every trace the app kept on your phone goes with it.
06Good to know

Children, security and changes

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them — we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Because your library, playlists and history never leave your phone, they're protected by your phone — its lock screen and Android's app sandbox. The requests the app does make (lyrics, ads, notices, purchases) travel over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you the right to access, correct or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none — so there's nothing for us to hand over or erase. For the data Google receives through ads and purchases, use Google's privacy controls.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something new with data, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes are announced in the app.
